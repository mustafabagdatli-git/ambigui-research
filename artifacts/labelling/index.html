How to judge

Judge only what's visible in the screenshot. Don't guess what's below the fold. Answer "unsure" when it really is a toss-up; it counts as no answer, not as half. Each answer saves as soon as you click it, and you can change it later.

Headline means the largest text that states what the page is about, usually the H1.
